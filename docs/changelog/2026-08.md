# August 2026

## 2026-08-04 — Scalable platform administration and three-tier billing

- **Change:** Replaced tenant scans/N+1 reads with indexed, cursor-paginated
  business projections and materialized platform metrics. Added payment status
  indexes, billing contacts, audited superadmin management with last-admin
  protection, and stored VAT-inclusive document components. Plans are now
  Biashara (KES 800; 1 store/5 users), Growth (KES 2,000; 3/10), and Plus
  (KES 5,000; 10/30). Billing and purchase-order emails now set the configured
  support mailbox as Reply-To. Upcoming charges are server-calculated for the
  current or scheduled plan, can be paid before trial/period expiry, and notify
  billing support on submission. Audited percentage offers are limited by
  confirmed eligible-payment count. Platform admins can create indexed,
  date-bound promotions targeted to new accounts, existing accounts, both
  audiences, and selected plans. Eligibility is revalidated when claimed and
  then snapshotted into the account billing audit trail. Existing accounts
  explicitly accept an eligible offer; an account may hold only one active
  promotion, and applying one never refunds or credits an already-paid period.
- **Product pricing:** Added admin-only, idempotent multi-unit price adjustments
  that apply immediately or at an exact future time without a scheduled worker.
  One future adjustment per product can be replaced or cancelled, all actions
  are audited, overlapping promotions are validated, and optimistic conditions
  prevent concurrent overwrites. Direct edits to existing selling-unit prices
  are rejected. Checkout accepts the catalog price reviewed by the cashier and
  returns a structured `PRICE_CHANGED` conflict before committing a stale
  basket; historical sale price snapshots remain unchanged. The additive
  DynamoDB fields require no backfill or destructive migration.
- **Annual billing:** Added a persisted monthly/annual subscription interval.
  Annual payments are server-calculated at 90% of twelve monthly payments,
  extend access for twelve calendar months, and retain their interval and
  coverage on payments and documents. Platform MRR normalizes annual revenue
  to a monthly value. Annual renewals receive seven days of grace versus one
  day for monthly renewals. Payments confirmed during annual grace preserve the
  original service period and renewal anniversary. Promotions may target
  monthly billing, annual billing, or both. An annual promotion applies after
  the standard 10% annual saving. New invoices and receipts immutably preserve
  separate plan-price, annual-discount, promotion-credit, and total amounts.
- **Maintenance:** Dependabot now checks npm and GitHub Actions weekly on
  Mondays. Operational changes are archived monthly with one compact entry per
  day.
- **Rollout:** The additive platform billing backfill completed for all existing
  businesses and its migration entry point has been retired. Configure vendor
  VAT from verified tax advice and verify pagination/search, payment review,
  metrics, plan limits, billing contacts, invitations, audits, and last-admin
  protection.
- **Recovery/status:** Roll back client and server together; retain projections,
  indexes, contacts, documents, and audits. Platform API reads remain
  side-effect-free and index-only. Reverting
  plan prices affects only future payments. Implementation and data backfill
  are complete; production tax review is pending. Deploy the server and public
  read-only promotion route before the client. No migration is required;
  disabling a promotion stops future visibility and claims without rewriting
  offers already assigned to accounts. Deploy this server before the annual
  billing client. Existing records safely resolve to monthly billing; rollback
  preserves annual payment periods already confirmed.

## 2026-08-03 — Tenant billing, generated codes, and BiasharaKit APIs

- **Change:** Added tenant plans/trials, manual M-Pesa submissions, immutable
  billing documents, enforcement and reminders, superadmin review/configuration
  APIs, audited standard-plan assignment, and complete tenant discovery without
  exposing operational data. Blank master-data codes now use tenant-scoped
  atomic sequences. Customer errors, emails, and the active-role header use
  BiasharaKit (`x-biasharakit-role`) exclusively.
- **Impact:** Existing tenant billing is initialized additively; generated code
  gaps after failed creates are harmless. Legacy tenants retain
  `legacy-pending-*` policy history rather than false acceptance, and standard
  plan assignment preserves payment/cancellation dates while clearing custom
  overrides after downgrade-limit validation.
- **Rollout:** The tenant billing backfill completed and its migration entry
  point has been retired. Provision a verified `superadmin`, verify all tenants,
  and only enable enforcement after legal/tax review and real vendor/Till
  configuration.
- **Verification/recovery/status:** Check trials, plan limits, restricted staff,
  exports, payments/receipts, worker invocation, platform configuration and
  audits, and generated codes. Disable enforcement to stop restrictions and
  scheduling; retain billing, document, audit, and sequence records. Production
  activation remains pending configuration, migration, and review.

## 2026-08-02 — Checkout controls, Kenyan VAT, and product-unit costing

- **Change:** Added server-enforced payment/customer/markdown policies with
  immutable price snapshots and atomic audits; Kenyan VAT, supplier invoices,
  partial payments, and 2% withholding-VAT estimates; and product-specific unit
  conversions with exact lot values.
- **Why/data:** Product-level conversions replace unreliable universal package
  assumptions. Existing checkout uses safe defaults, historical transaction and
  purchase snapshots remain unchanged, and no historic tax reconstruction is
  attempted.
- **Rollout:** The product-unit backfill completed for every existing workspace
  and its migration entry point has been retired.
- **Verification:** Test disabled payments, required customers, markdown caps
  and VAT recalculation; mixed-rate receiving, sales, Money totals, partial
  payments and withholding splits; representative units, supplier links, draft
  purchase orders, and lot-value totals.
- **Recovery/status:** Removing checkout settings restores safe defaults;
  disabling VAT affects only future transactions. Correct/deactivate bad units
  and relink future supplier orders rather than rewriting history. Active until
  every existing workspace is migrated and verified; this release does not
  submit to eTIMS or file/remit taxes.
